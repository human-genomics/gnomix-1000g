Local ancestry of the 1000 Genomes panel

A karyogram and global ancestry for every one of the 3,202 high-coverage samples, from the published pretrained Gnomix model with Gnofix phase correction.

Code and method · Download all outputs

Karyograms drawn in the style of ancestry_pipeline. Where Martin et al. (2017) published a karyogram for the same sample, it is linked beside ours: their calls come from RFMix with three ancestries (African, European, Native American) and mask windows below 0.9 posterior, while ours come from Gnomix with eight ancestries and are not masked, so the two differ in resolution and in the number of labels available.

Martin AR, Gignoux CR, Walters RK, et al. Human demographic history impacts genetic risk prediction across diverse populations. Am J Hum Genet 100, 635-649 (2017). Byrska-Bishop M, et al. Cell 185, 3426-3440 (2022). Hilmarsson H, et al. Nat Commun 17, 9070 (2026).
